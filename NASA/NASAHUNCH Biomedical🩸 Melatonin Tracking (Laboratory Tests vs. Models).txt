[Student Body Sensor] ➔ [Modular Board / Kit] ➔ [Raspberry Pi / Arduino] ➔ [PC Browser Dashboard via Python or GUI]


This configuration keeps student research independent from standard vendor app limitations, ensuring that all physical data is extracted into open, file-accessible formats. [4]
To tailor these options to your class or lab, please let me know:
* * What specific bio-metrics are the students trying to isolate? (e.g., muscle fatigue, continuous heart rates, brainwave spikes)
* Do they prefer a fully pre-assembled kit with its own software (like BITalino), or a custom build project using loose parts?
